Candidate Tips Page 7

Four things that help people do well

Actionable advice for your upcoming conversation

1 Revisit the role

Pick 2–3 key outcomes that matter most to this position and reflect on how you’d tackle them from day one.

2 Bring real, concrete examples

Structure thoughts around the situation, what decisions you made, and measurable impact. Specific beats polished.

3 Try what we build

Plan a stay on HomeToGo or explore our mobile app. Note one feature you loved and one opportunity for improvement.

4 Bring your honest questions

The interview is a two-way dialogue! Ask about team dynamics, product strategy, technical debt, or leadership culture.
